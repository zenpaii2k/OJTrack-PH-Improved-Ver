Our Story

Founded in 2026 by the NOUS R&D Team, OJTrack PH was born out of a need for more transparent and efficient OJT monitoring in Philippine schools. What started as a small project has evolved into a comprehensive platform serving multiple disciplines.

Our mission is to provide students with a reliable digital footprint of their internship growth while giving coordinators the tools they need to verify progress with zero paperwork.
